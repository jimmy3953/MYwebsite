Co-curricular Activities

Explore opportunities beyond the classroom that build teamwork, leadership, creativity and confidence.

CLUB

Robotics Club

Hands-on projects develop problem-solving, programming and creative thinking.

CLUB

Coding Club

Students explore web development, programming concepts and digital projects.

UNIFORMED

Leadership, outdoor skills, teamwork and community service are developed through practical activities.

COMPETITION

SOCIETY

PARTICIPATION

Opportunities for every learner

Students are encouraged to participate according to their interests, abilities and schedules. Activity advisers provide guidance and promote respectful, inclusive participation.

  • Regular club and training sessions
  • Inter-school competitions and events
  • Leadership and service opportunities
  • Opportunities to represent the school

Typical Weekly Schedule

Tuesday: Sports training · 3:00–4:30 PM

Wednesday: Clubs & societies · 3:00–4:30 PM

Thursday: Uniformed bodies · 3:00–4:30 PM

Friday: Competition / project preparation · 2:30–4:00 PM

Schedules may change according to school events and competition dates.
