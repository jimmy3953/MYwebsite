About the School

Learn about our background, identity, vision, mission, educational objectives, leadership and daily operations.

OUR BACKGROUND

Learning with purpose

Horizon International School was established to provide a supportive and engaging learning environment where students can develop academically, socially and personally.

The school combines a strong academic foundation with practical learning, co-curricular participation, leadership development and pastoral support. Our approach encourages students to become confident, responsible and lifelong learners.

  • Student-centred and inclusive learning environment
  • Strong communication between school and families
  • Balanced academic and co-curricular development
  • Safe, respectful and supportive school culture

A welcoming school community

Our spaces and programmes are planned to help students learn confidently and participate actively.

Vision, Mission & Educational Objectives

Our identity is built around academic growth, character and community.
